# Code of Conduct

CHEEKU INFINITY welcomes respectful participation from people of all experience levels.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful, constructive and considerate.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence, not personal attacks.
- Credit original authors and respect project licenses.
- Do not post secrets, private data, malware or content intended to harm people or systems.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. The maintainer may remove violating content or restrict participation.

Report concerns privately using SECURITY.md.
